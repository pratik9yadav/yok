# `train.jsonl`: where it comes from and its licence

`data/train.jsonl` (1,300 rows) is **generated** by `make_data.py` (`python make_data.py`; the same file
comes out every time). The shopkeeper replies are written from templates, but they are built on real,
openly licensed data from `data/real/` (sources, licences and full attribution: `data/real/NOTICE.md`):

| What in `train.jsonl` | Comes from | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Product names (shortened, as a caller would ask), MRP and selling price | `products_flipkart.jsonl`: Flipkart Products by PromptCloud (Kaggle) | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Product names (only the Latin brand/model words of the title) and prices | `products_amazon_in.jsonl`: Amazon India Products 2023 by asaniczka (Kaggle) | ODC-By |
| Prices said in Hindi and Marathi words (formal, colloquial like डेढ़ सौ / दीडशे, hundreds style, romanized) | `price_words.json`: AI4Bharat indic-numtowords (MIT), Unicode CLDR (Unicode licence), gpu-sankhya (MIT), Google Dakshina (CC BY-SA 4.0) | CC BY-SA 4.0 as a whole; see `data/real/NOTICE-price_words.md` |
| Latin spellings of Hindi and Marathi words (nahi / nhi / nahin, ahe / aahe, ...) with their weights | `romanization_variants.json`: Google Dakshina and COMI-LINGUA (lingo-iitgn, IIT Gandhinagar) | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

## Licence of `train.jsonl`

## What was changed
- Product names were shortened to what a caller would say ("Castrol 10W-40 engine oil"). From Amazon
  titles only the Latin brand/model words are kept; the machine-translated Hindi is dropped. Products a
  hardware, electrical or auto-parts shop would not sell were left out, and so were online-only prices.
- Online prices that look like an accessory, a spare part or a multi-pack (an ₹245 "angle grinder", a ₹722
  "9W LED bulb") were left out, by price limits per kind of product.
- A real product's price moves by up to 10% from row to row (a shop's price, not the online one), so a
  product name does not give away its price. MRP-vs-offered replies use real MRP / selling price pairs
  (only pairs with a shop-like discount), moved by the same amount.
- A price said in words is rounded to the nearest price that `price_words.json` has words for. The
  hundreds style for 2100-4550 (पच्चीस सौ, पंचवीसशे, "pachis sau pachas") is not in `price_words.json`
  (it has it only for 1100-1999); `make_data.py` builds it from the json's formal words by the same rule.
- Words in romanized replies are swapped for other attested spellings, by their weights. Chat shorthand
  ("h" for hai, "nhi", "gya", "khtm") and spellings that are not words are not used: the replies stand
  for speech-to-text output.
- In some Devanagari replies the alternative is written in Devanagari (a small word list in
  `make_data.py`, e.g. हॅवेल्स फॅन), and its label is written the same way.

## What is not in it
- `data/real/xpqa_hi_examples.jsonl` (xPQA, CDLA-Sharing-1.0) is **not** in `train.jsonl`; it is added
  separately at training time.
- `data/test.jsonl` and `data/hard_test.jsonl` were not used to make the data. `make_data.py` reads them
  only at the end, to check that no training reply equals a test reply or shares 8 words in a row with
  one (a test in `test_training.py` checks the same).
- No personal data, and no phone numbers.
